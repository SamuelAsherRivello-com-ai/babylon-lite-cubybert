# Proposal

## Why
The fixed portrait cabinet wastes space on modern screens and devotes substantial height to decoration.

## What Changes
- Fit the cabinet within portrait ratios from 9:20 to 10:16.
- Remove the marquee and bottom strip, move the compact title into the control panel, and preserve the game view proportions with minimal horizontal gaps.
- Verify gameplay, responsive layout, and visible controls.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `portrait-presentation`: Flexible portrait frame and compact cabinet layout.

## Impact
Cabinet markup, CSS, renderer sizing, and browser tests.
